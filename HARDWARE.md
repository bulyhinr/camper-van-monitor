## 🔍 Component Details & Sourcing Notes

### 1. E-Paper Screen & Controller
* **Why Waveshare 7.5" V2:** High resolution (800×480), zero power consumption when static, perfectly legible in direct sunlight, no glare at night.
* **Controller:** The **Waveshare Universal e-Paper Driver Board** integrates the ESP32, USB-UART programmer, and the E-Ink power boost circuitry directly onto a single PCB with a dedicated FFC clip connector. Highly recommended over bare ESP32 DevKit to avoid complex manual level-shifting.

### 2. LiFePO4 BMS (JBD / Xiaoxiang)
* Supports single battery banks or multi-pack parallel setups (1 to 4 packs, 12V / 24V / 48V, any capacity e.g. 100Ah, 200Ah, 300Ah+).
* ESPHome connects via BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy) without wires, polling SoC %, voltage, current, power, and capacity every 5 seconds.

### 3. Safety Sensors (MQ-9 and MQ-2)
* **Pre-heating burn-in:** Brand new MQ sensors require a **24–48 hour burn-in period** (keep powered at 5V) before the semiconductor resistance stabilizes.
* **Sensitivity adjustment:** Use the onboard blue potentiometer with a small screwdriver. In clean ambient air, rotate until the onboard DO LED just turns off.

### 4. MC-38 Magnetic Reed Contacts
* Place the wired switch part on the camper van roof frame and the magnet part on the moving roof hatch lid.
* Alignment tolerance is typically 15–25 mm.

## 📦 Enclosure & Installation Advice

1. **Inclinometer Mounting:** Fix the MPU-6050 firmly to the vehicle chassis or a rigid panel with components facing **UP** and X-arrow pointing **FORWARD**. Avoid flexible foam tape that could vibrate or sag over time.
2. **Thermal Isolation:** Keep the DS18B20 indoor temperature probe isolated from heat generated by the ESP32 voltage regulator or MQ sensor heating coils.
3. **MQ Sensor Airflow:** Enclosure walls around the MQ gas sensors must feature ventilation slots so air circulates freely across the sensor mesh.
